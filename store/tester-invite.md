## Message (WhatsApp or email)

Hi! I've made a chess app called Chess Coach and it's nearly ready for the Play Store. Could you try it for about a week before it goes live?

What to do:
1. Open this link on your Android phone, tap "Accept invite", then install the app from the Play Store: [TEST LINK]
2. Use it a few times over the week. Please try each of these once:
   - the level check when you first open it
   - a lesson
   - a few puzzles, and tap Hint on one
   - a game against the computer, then its review
   - another piece set (in Settings) and the 3D view (the button above the board)
3. Send me at least one comment: what you liked, or what was confusing.

It's free and needs no sign-up. You'll see a small test ad banner now and then; that is expected.

If anything looks wrong or confusing, reply to this message. Thank you!
