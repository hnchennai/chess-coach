## Message (WhatsApp or email)

Hi! I've made a chess app called Chess Coach, and Google needs 12 people to test it for 14 days before it can go on the Play Store. Could you help?

What to do:
1. Join this group with your Google account (the one on your Android phone): [GROUP LINK]
2. Open this link on your phone and tap "Become a tester", then install the app from the Play Store: [TEST LINK]
3. Keep it installed for at least 14 days, and open it a few times a week.
4. Over those two weeks, please try each of these once:
   - the level check when you first open it
   - a lesson
   - a few puzzles, and tap Hint on one
   - a game against the computer, then its review
   - another piece set (in Settings) and the 3D view (the button above the board)
5. Send me at least one comment: what you liked, or what was confusing.

It's free and needs no sign-up. You'll see a small test ad banner now and then; that is expected.

If anything looks wrong or confusing, reply to this message or use "Send feedback" on the app's Play Store page. Thank you!
